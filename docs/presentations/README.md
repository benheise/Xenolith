| **Length** | 20 slides: about 35 minutes of talk, then questions |

A conference talk on why software transparency matters. It covers:

- the incidents that got us here (Heartbleed, Equifax, SolarWinds, Log4Shell, libwebp and xz utils);
- how SBOM rules differ between sectors and why almost none require publication;
- why a vendor's paperwork is hard to check against what actually ships;
- the companies that already publish SBOMs, VEX and CSAF advisories, and why that puts them ahead;
- what Xenolith does about it, and a call to action for vendors, buyers and regulators.

Every figure is cited on its slide. The full source list is on slides 18 and 19, and the document control and notices are on slide 20. The figures about Xenolith's own scans come from [`design/xenolith-scan.md`](../../design/xenolith-scan.md#real-artifact-proofs-november-2026).

## Changing the deck

Edit the `.pptx` in PowerPoint. The deck uses a theme with named layouts and placeholders, so its colours and fonts can be changed in one place. After an edit:

1. Export a new PDF.
2. Raise the version on the title slide, in the footer and in the version history on slide 20.
3. Update this table.

Facts in the deck are current as of the version date.
